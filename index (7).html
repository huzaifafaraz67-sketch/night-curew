STORY

apartment 4B, Kessler Row — two weeks after

Leo Vance is nine. Since Dad left, Mom works the 11 PM to 6 AM shift at the packing plant, so the nights belong to Leo and the long hallway outside his kitchen.

Two weeks ago Ada from 4C was babysitting. At 2 AM someone knocked and said it was Mom. Ada opened the door. The police found her shoes in the hallway, still laced, pointing at the wall.

Grandma taped a sheet of notebook paper to the fridge and made Mom promise to read it out loud every night. The thing in the hallway cannot come inside on its own — it has to be invited. So it practises. It copies voices, it knocks politely, and it waits for you to look at it. The moment it knows you can see it, it stops pretending.

Tonight there is no babysitter. Six hours, five rules, one flashlight with a dying battery.

Mom's shift ends at 6:00 AM. Stay inside. Stay on the list.

THE MIDNIGHT RULES

taped to the fridge door — Grandma's handwriting
  1. If the hallway light is ON, do not look down the hallway.
  2. Mom comes home at 6:00 AM. Anyone knocking before then is not Mom.
  3. If you hear your own name from the kitchen, hide in the closet until it stops.
  4. Never open the fridge twice in the same hour.
  5. Whatever is in the hallway must not learn that you can see it.
Leo — I love you. Just do the list. I'll be home before sunrise.

HOW TO PLAY

survive from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM
  1. WASD to move, SHIFT to run, mouse to look. ESC releases the mouse.
  2. [F] toggles the flashlight. The battery drains and flickers when low.
  3. [E] interacts with anything highlighted within reach: fridge, notes, light switch, TV, phone, closet, front door.
  4. NERVE drops while you break a rule and slowly recovers when you behave. At zero, the night ends badly.
  5. When it calls your name, get into the closet and press [E]. Come out only when it goes quiet.
  6. Never open the front door before 6:00 AM. At exactly 6:00 AM, open it.
Tip: the TV masks sound and adds light, but light attracts attention.
