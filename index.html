STORY

apartment 4B, Kessler Row — night one of the curfew

Leo Vance is nine. Since Dad left, Mom takes the 11 PM to 6 AM shift at the packing plant, and Kessler Row empties out after dark. 4C, 4D, 4E, 4F: condemned since the fire, doors still locked, hallway lights still wired to Leo's switch.

Two weeks ago Ada from 4C was babysitting — the last person on the floor besides them. At 2 AM someone knocked and said it was Mom. Ada opened the door. The police found her shoes in the hallway, still laced, pointing at the wall.

Grandma taped a sheet of notebook paper to the fridge and made Mom promise Leo would read it out loud every night. The thing in the hallway cannot come in on its own — it has to be let in. So it practises. It knocks politely. It learns voices from behind the doors: the delivery man, Mom, Ada, and now Leo. It has learned one sentence perfectly and says it through every locked door on the floor — “I will find you.” The moment it knows you can see it, it stops pretending to be a person.

Tonight nobody is babysitting. Seven hours, five rules, one flashlight with a dying battery, and a cupboard with a latch on the inside.

The list is not superstition. Every line is a door held shut. Wash the plate so the kitchen has no reason to call your name. Draw the curtain so nothing at the window can watch you sleep. Kill the hallway light so you are never tempted to look. Turn the deadbolt so the only knock that matters is Mom's, at six. Do the list, and the night cannot find a way in. Miss one, and it will practise on you until it does.

11:00 PM — the deadbolt turns from the outside. Stay inside. Stay on the list. It is already saying your name.

THE MIDNIGHT RULES

taped to the fridge door — Grandma's handwriting
  1. If the hallway light is ON, do not look down the hallway.
  2. Mom comes home at 6:00 AM. Anyone knocking before then is not Mom.
  3. If you hear your own name from the kitchen, hide in the closet until it stops.
  4. Never open the fridge twice in the same hour.
  5. Whatever is in the hallway must not learn that you can see it.
  6. Keep the curtain shut after the knocking starts. Do not go to the window to look out. What is out there wants your face, and the glass will not stop it twice.
  7. Finish the list before you sleep: plate washed, curtain shut, TV and hall light off, deadbolt over.
  8. Inside the cupboard, latch the doors. A latched door is a door it cannot open.
Leo — I love you. Just do the list. I'll be home before sunrise.

HOW TO PLAY

survive from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM — 30 real seconds = 2 minutes on the clock
  1. WASD to move, SHIFT to run, mouse to look. ESC releases the mouse.
  2. [F] toggles the flashlight. The battery drains and flickers when low.
  3. [E] interacts with anything highlighted within reach: fridge, notes, light switch, TV, phone, cupboard, front door.
  4. The clock waits for the story: it stops at 11:04 PM until you read the rules, and again two minutes later until you eat on the couch.
  5. You cannot walk through furniture. The cupboard is the only safe place in the flat.
  6. NERVE drops while you break a rule and recovers when you behave. At zero, the night ends badly.
  7. After 5:00 AM it knocks four times. Each time: get in the cupboard, stay in for six seconds. Then find out why Mom never came.
  8. Never open the front door before 6:00 AM. At 6:00 AM, open it.
  9. PRACTICE MODE: same flat, no monster, frozen clock, nothing can kill you.
Tip: the TV masks sound and adds light, but light attracts attention.
